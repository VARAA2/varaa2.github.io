NNA Bots

NNA Bots are small private Telegram bots used by NNA for its own media library.

  • NNA Prompt Bot files images and prompts from Telegram into NNA's own Notion workspace and reads Google Docs that NNA links in its posts.
  • NNA Library files photos and videos posted in NNA's own Telegram group into NNA's own Google Drive folders and Notion pages.

The bots work only for NNA's own accounts. They are not a public service and do not accept sign-ups.
