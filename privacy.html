Privacy policy — NNA Bots

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who uses the app

The Google app "NNA Prompt Bot" is used only by NNA (nnaenthiranbot@gmail.com) for NNA's own Telegram bots. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data the app uses

  • Google Docs (read only): to read the text of Google Docs that NNA links in its own Telegram posts and copy that text into NNA's own Notion pages.
  • Google Drive: to upload NNA's own photos and videos into folders in NNA's own Drive, to make folders and shortcuts there, and to rename those files.

How the data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on NNA's own private server, readable only by the bot. Files and text go only to NNA's own Google Drive and NNA's own Notion workspace. Nothing is sold, shared with others, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
